Processing (direct pixel extraction, 2026-11-11):
- Keep the photographed grain, fibers and color details.
- Subtract broad illumination with a 96-source-pixel Gaussian blur.
- Reconcile opposite edges by periodic-plus-smooth decomposition.
- Scale local shadow/highlight relief (fine: 1.0/0.6; classic: 0.85/0.6).
- Soften the classic relief with a periodic Gaussian filter (sigma 1 source
  pixel), then reduce contrast while preserving each pixel's color direction:
  M = max(abs(R), abs(G), abs(B)); scale = 10 * tanh(0.5 * M / 10) / M.
  Recenter the softened relief before converting it back to RGBA. This limits
  prominent dark fibers and fragments as well as raised white fiber highlights.
- Center the local relief on the existing game paper color #f3efe7.
- Convert that relief into per-pixel RGBA using the minimum alpha needed to
  reconstruct it over #f3efe7; the paper substrate becomes transparent.
- Original-resolution PNG masters: 2048 x 1365.
- Runtime tiles: 1024 x 683, Lanczos reduction and lossless RGBA WebP.

Use normal compositing at opacity 1 and the tile's intrinsic dimensions.
